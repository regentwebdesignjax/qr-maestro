## 1. About QR Sensei

Whether you need a simple static code for a flyer or a fully branded dynamic code with real-time analytics and lead capture, QR Sensei has you covered. ## 2. QR Code Basics

**Q: What is a QR code?**
A: A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that any smartphone camera can scan to instantly open a website, save a contact, connect to Wi-Fi, and more. QR Sensei lets you generate, style, and manage these codes in one place.

**Q: What is the difference between a static and a dynamic QR code?**
A: 
- **Static QR codes** encode all the information directly into the code pattern. Once printed, the destination cannot be changed. They work offline (e.g., Wi-Fi passwords, plain text). The pattern is more dense/complex when the content is long.
- **Dynamic QR codes** encode only a short redirect link into the pattern. QR Sensei's servers forward every scan to your real destination, which you can change at any time in the dashboard — no reprinting needed. Dynamic codes also enable scan analytics.

**Q: What types of content can a QR code link to?**
A: QR Sensei supports 15+ content types:

| Content Type | Description | Plan Required |
|---|---|---|
| URL / Website | Any web link | White Belt & up |
| Digital Business Card | Rich profile with contact info, photo, social links | Black Belt |
| Linkpage | Branded landing page with multiple links | Black Belt |
| Business Page | Business info with hours, map, contact | Black Belt |
| vCard Contact | Downloadable contact card (.vcf) | White Belt & up |
| Wi-Fi Credentials | Auto-connect to a network | White Belt & up |
| Plain Text | Any text message | White Belt & up |
| Coupon Code | Promo code with description and redemption URL | White Belt & up |
| PDF | Display a PDF file | White Belt & up |
| Image | Display an image | White Belt & up |
| MP3 / Audio | Play an audio file | White Belt & up |
| Social Media | Links to up to 11 social platforms | White Belt & up |
| Phone Call | Tap to call a number | White Belt & up |
| SMS | Tap to send a text message | White Belt & up |
| Map Location | Show a location on a map | Black Belt |

**Q: Can I change the content a QR code points to without creating a new one?**
A: Yes — but only if it is a **dynamic** QR code (Black Belt plan). Edit the code in The Dojo, update the destination, and every future scan instantly goes to the new content. Your printed materials stay valid. Static codes cannot be changed after creation.

**Q: Do QR codes expire?**
A: QR Sensei codes do not expire on their own. A dynamic code will continue to redirect as long as your Black Belt subscription is active. If a subscription lapses, dynamic codes may become unreachable until the subscription is restored.

**Q: How do I scan a QR Sensei code?**
A: Open the camera app on any modern smartphone (iOS or Android) and point it at the code. Most devices detect QR codes automatically without a separate app. You can also use any dedicated QR scanner app.

**Q: How many QR codes can I create?**
A:
- **White Belt (free):** Up to 10 static QR codes
- **Black Belt (paid):** Unlimited static and dynamic QR codes

## 5. Customization & Design

**Q: Can I customize how my QR code looks?**
A: Yes — QR Sensei offers extensive design options:
- **Foreground color** — the color of the QR modules (dots/squares)
- **Background color** — the color behind the modules
- **Gradients** — apply a linear or radial gradient using a secondary color
- **Module style** — choose Squares, Dots, or Rounded modules
- **Eye (finder pattern) shape** — the three corner squares can be Square, Circle, or Rounded, independently for the outer and inner parts
- **Eye color** — set a custom color for the three finder eyes, separate from the module color
- **Logo / image embed** — upload your company logo to appear in the center of the QR code
- **Transparent background** — export with no background for overlaying on colored materials

**Q: Can I add my logo to a QR code?**
A: Yes. When creating or editing a QR code, upload your logo image in the design section. The logo will be centered inside the code. QR Sensei automatically adjusts the error-correction level to ensure the code remains scannable even with a logo covering part of it. You can also add a white background padding around the logo for better contrast.

**Q: Does customization affect whether the QR code can be scanned?**
A: Light customization (colors, gradients, rounded modules) has minimal impact. Adding a logo reduces scanning reliability slightly, which is why QR Sensei uses high error correction. Extremely low contrast between foreground and background (e.g., light gray on white) can make codes harder to scan — always test your design before printing.

**Q: Can I use a transparent background on my QR code?**
A: Yes. Toggle the transparent background option in the design settings. This is especially useful when you want to place the code directly on a colored surface or overlay it on a design without a white box.

**Q: Are design options available on the free White Belt plan?**
A: White Belt users can change basic foreground and background colors. Advanced features — gradients, logo embedding, custom eye shapes and colors — are Black Belt exclusive.

**Q: What resolution are downloaded QR codes?**
A: PNG downloads are 1024 × 1024 pixels, suitable for most print and digital uses. SVG downloads are infinitely scalable with no quality loss, ideal for large-format printing like banners and signage.

## 6. Dynamic QR Codes

**Q: What is a dynamic QR code?**
A: A dynamic QR code encodes only a short redirect link inside the pattern. When someone scans it, QR Sensei's servers instantly forward them to your real destination (URL, business card, linkpage, etc.). Because only the short link is baked into the QR pattern, you can update the destination at any time — the printed code never needs to change.

**Q: Why should I use a dynamic QR code instead of a static one?**
A: Dynamic codes offer:
1. **Flexibility** — update the destination without reprinting
2. **Analytics** — track every scan (who, when, where, on what device)
3. **Custom domains** — brand the redirect URL with your own subdomain
4. **Rich content types** — Business Cards, Linkpages, Business Pages, and Map Locations require dynamic codes

**Q: How do I create a dynamic QR code?**
A: In the "Strike a New Code" flow, select any content type that supports dynamic mode (URL, Business Card, Linkpage, Business Page, Map Location). Dynamic is the default for those types on Black Belt. Static codes are limited to simpler content types like plain text and vCard.

**Q: Can I change a static QR code to dynamic, or vice versa?**
A: No. The static/dynamic type is set at creation and cannot be changed. If you need to switch, create a new code of the desired type.

**Q: Can I update where a dynamic QR code points to?**
A: Yes. Go to The Dojo, find the code, click **Edit**, update the destination (URL, business card info, linkpage content, etc.), and save. Changes take effect immediately — no reprinting needed.

**Q: Are dynamic QR codes available on the free plan?**
A: No. Dynamic QR codes require a Black Belt (paid) subscription.

**Q: What happens to dynamic QR codes if I cancel my subscription?**
A: If your Black Belt subscription lapses, dynamic codes become inactive. Scans will not redirect until you restore your subscription. Static codes remain usable but cannot be changed.

## 7. Analytics & Tracking

**Q: What analytics does QR Sensei provide?**
A: Black Belt users get real-time scan analytics for every dynamic QR code:
- **Total scan count** and estimated unique scanners
- **Geographic breakdown** — country, region (state/province), and city, visualized on an interactive map
- **Device analytics** — mobile, tablet, or desktop
- **OS analytics** — iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux
- **Browser analytics** — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Opera
- **Time-based analytics** — scans over time (line chart), peak hours heatmap
- **Date range filtering** — preset ranges (Today, Yesterday, Last 7/14/30/60/90 days) and extended ranges (Last 12/24 months, Lifetime), plus a custom date picker

**Q: Is analytics available on the free White Belt plan?**
A: No. Scan analytics is a Black Belt exclusive feature.

**Q: How are "unique scanners" counted?**
A: Unique scanners are estimated by combining browser, device type, and country information from each scan. Because QR Sensei does not use cookies or require sign-in from scanners, this is an approximation rather than a definitive unique-user count.

**Q: What geographic data is captured?**
A: Each scan records country, state/region, and city when detectable. This data is plotted on an interactive map inside the Analytics view so you can see scan density at a glance.

**Q: Can I see what time of day people scan my codes?**
A: Yes. The time-of-day heatmap shows your peak scanning hours so you can plan campaigns and promotions around when your audience is most active.

**Q: How do I export my analytics data?**
A: From the Analytics view, you can:
- **Export CSV** — raw scan records with timestamp, location, device, OS, browser, and referrer
- **Export PDF** — formatted report with charts, statistics, header, and QR Sensei branding

**Q: How far back does analytics history go?**
A: Analytics are stored indefinitely. You can filter to any custom date range or choose "Lifetime" to see all data since the code was created.

**Q: Does QR Sensei track individual people who scan my codes?**
A: No personally identifiable information is collected about scanners. Scan records include device and approximate location metadata but no names, email addresses, or user accounts.

## 10. Custom Domains

**Q: What is the Custom Domain add-on?**
A: The Custom Domain add-on lets you use your own branded subdomain for your QR code redirect URLs. Instead of a QR Sensei domain, your codes redirect through something like `qr.yourbrand.com`. This reinforces brand trust, especially when code URLs are visible on packaging or marketing materials.

**Q: How much does the Custom Domain add-on cost?**
A:
- **Monthly plan add-on:** +$19/month
- **Annual plan add-on:** +$190/year

**Q: How do I set up a custom domain?**
A:
1. Purchase the Custom Domain add-on (at checkout or through the billing portal).
2. Go to **Custom Domains** in The Dojo and enter your desired subdomain (e.g., `qr.yourbrand.com`).
3. Log in to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, etc.) and add a **CNAME DNS record** pointing your subdomain to `customers.qr-sensei.com`.
4. Return to QR Sensei and click **Check Status** (or Refresh). DNS propagation usually completes within 5–30 minutes.
5. Once active, all new dynamic QR codes you create will automatically use your custom domain.

**Q: What type of domain can I use — a full domain or a subdomain?**
A: Only **subdomains** are supported (e.g., `qr.yourbrand.com`, `codes.yourcompany.com`). Root/apex domains (e.g., `yourbrand.com`) are not compatible with the CNAME setup.

**Q: Is SSL included with a custom domain?**
A: Yes. QR Sensei automatically provisions and renews an SSL certificate for your custom subdomain via Cloudflare. Your QR redirect URLs will use `https://`.

**Q: How long does DNS propagation take?**
A: Typically 5–30 minutes, though it can take up to 48 hours in rare cases depending on your DNS provider.

**Q: What are the possible statuses for my custom domain?**
A: 
- **Pending** — DNS records are not yet verified. Click Refresh to recheck.
- **Active** — Domain is live, SSL is provisioned, and QR codes are routing through it.
- **Deactivated** — The domain has been removed or the add-on was cancelled.

**Q: Can I have more than one custom domain?**
A: Each account supports one custom domain associated with the add-on. Contact support if you have an enterprise need for multiple domains.

**Q: What happens to my custom domain if I cancel the add-on?**
A: The domain is immediately deactivated and removed from Cloudflare routing. Existing QR codes that used the custom domain will stop redirecting through it. Contact support for assistance transitioning to the default domain.

**Q: Do existing QR codes automatically use my new custom domain?**
A: New dynamic QR codes created after the domain is active will use your custom domain by default. Existing codes retain their original redirect URL. You can recreate existing codes to use the new domain.

## 12. File Downloads & Exports

**Q: In what formats can I download my QR codes?**
A:
- **PNG** — 1024 × 1024 pixel raster image. Best for digital use (websites, email, social media) and standard print sizes. Supports transparent backgrounds.
- **SVG** — Scalable vector format. Best for large-format printing (banners, signage, merchandise) where you need perfect sharpness at any size. Logos are embedded as base64; gradients and all design customizations are preserved.

**Q: Which download format should I use for printing?**
A: Use **SVG** for professional printing and large formats — it scales to any size without pixelation. Use **PNG** for digital distribution, social media, and standard document embedding.

**Q: Can I export my scan analytics?**
A: Yes (Black Belt only):
- **CSV export** — every raw scan record with timestamp, country, city, device type, OS, browser, and referrer
- **PDF export** — formatted analytics report with charts and key statistics, ready to share with clients or stakeholders

**Q: Can I export my leads?**
A: Yes. From the Leads section, export all captured leads as a **CSV** with columns: Name, Email, Phone, Source Card, Lead Tag, Notes, and Date.

## 16. Troubleshooting

**Q: My QR code is not scanning. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps:
1. **Check contrast** — ensure there is enough color difference between the foreground and background (dark on light, or light on dark). Avoid similar shades.
2. **Check size** — QR codes need to be large enough for cameras to resolve. For print, aim for at least 1 inch × 1 inch (2.5 cm × 2.5 cm). Larger is better for complex codes with logos.
3. **Check damage** — if printed, ensure the code is not smudged, torn, or obscured.
4. **Test on multiple devices** — try scanning with a different phone or camera app.
5. **Re-download the code** — download a fresh PNG or SVG from QR Sensei and test with the digital version first.
6. **Check if it is active** — in The Dojo, confirm the code's status is active (not deactivated).

**Q: My custom domain status is "Pending" and not activating. What should I do?**
A: 
1. Confirm you added the CNAME record correctly at your domain registrar: the subdomain (e.g., `qr`) should point to `customers.qr-sensei.com`.
2. Allow 5–30 minutes for DNS propagation (sometimes up to 48 hours).
3. Click the **Refresh / Check Status** button in QR Sensei to recheck.
4. Use a DNS lookup tool (e.g., `dig qr.yourbrand.com CNAME` in terminal, or an online DNS checker) to verify the record is visible globally.
5. If it remains stuck after 48 hours, contact QR Sensei support.

**Q: I hit my QR code limit on the free plan. What should I do?**
A: White Belt is limited to 10 static QR codes. To create more codes or unlock dynamic codes, upgrade to Black Belt. Alternatively, delete unused codes to free up slots within the 10-code limit.

**Q: My analytics show no data even though the code has been scanned. Why?**
A: Analytics only records scans of **dynamic** QR codes on **Black Belt** accounts. If you are on a free plan, or if your code is static, no analytics data is collected. Also ensure you are looking at the correct date range — the default may exclude older scans.

**Q: I cannot see the Leads section. Why?**
A: The Leads section is a Black Belt feature. Upgrade to Black Belt to access lead capture and management.

**Q: My HubSpot sync shows an error. What should I do?**
A: Common causes:
- **Not connected:** Reconnect HubSpot in your integration settings.
- **Missing permissions:** The HubSpot connection needs `crm.contacts.write` and `crm.lists.read` scopes. Reconnect and ensure you authorize all requested permissions.
- **Contact conflict:** HubSpot may reject duplicate contacts in some configurations. QR Sensei attempts to update existing contacts by email — if this fails, check HubSpot for duplicate entries.
If the issue persists, contact QR Sensei support with the error message shown.

**Q: I accidentally deleted a QR code. Can I recover it?**
A: QR codes are soft-deleted (marked inactive) in most cases. Contact support promptly — recovery may be possible depending on how the deletion was performed.

**Q: My Linkpage URL slug says it is already taken. What can I do?**
A: Slugs are unique across all QR Sensei users. Try a variation — for example, add your brand name or a number suffix (e.g., `yourname-2` or `yourbrand-links`).

**Q: The QR code downloads look blurry when printed at large sizes.**
A: Download the **SVG** version instead of PNG. SVG is a vector format that scales to any size without quality loss. Use SVG for anything larger than roughly 4 × 4 inches (10 × 10 cm).

**Q: I cannot log in to my account.**
A: Try the following:
1. Confirm you are using the correct email address.
2. Use the "Forgot Password" / account recovery flow.
3. Clear your browser cache and try again.
4. Try a different browser or incognito mode.
5. Contact QR Sensei support if none of the above work.

## 17. Security & Privacy

**Q: Is QR Sensei secure?**
A: Yes. QR Sensei is built with enterprise-grade security:
- All data is transmitted over **HTTPS/TLS** encryption
- Custom domain SSL certificates are automatically provisioned and renewed
- Payments are processed by **Stripe**, which is PCI-DSS compliant — QR Sensei never stores your card details
- Authentication is managed by a secure OAuth-based system
- Database access uses service-role isolation; user data is scoped to the authenticated account

**Q: What data does QR Sensei collect about people who scan my QR codes?**
A: Scan records capture:
- Approximate geographic location (country, region, city — derived from IP address)
- Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Operating system and browser
- Referrer URL (if available)
- Timestamp (UTC)

No names, email addresses, or personal identifiers are collected from scanners unless they voluntarily submit a lead form on a Digital Business Card page.

**Q: Does QR Sensei sell my data?**
A: No. QR Sensei does not sell your data or scanner data to third parties.

**Q: Is scanner data stored permanently?**
A: Scan data is stored and available through the analytics dashboard indefinitely. Refer to QR Sensei's Privacy Policy for full data retention details.

**Q: Where is my data hosted?**
A: QR Sensei runs on Cloudflare's global edge network (for routing) and a managed backend platform. Data residency details are available in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

**Q: Is QR Sensei GDPR compliant?**
A: QR Sensei is designed with privacy best practices in mind. For specific GDPR compliance questions relevant to your use case (e.g., if you are scanning codes in the EU), please review the Privacy Policy or contact support.
